### 1. Factual Gameplay Statistics are Non-Copyrightable
Under the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 102(b)) and the Supreme Court landmark ruling in *Feist Publications, Inc. v. Rural Telephone Service Co.*, 499 U.S. 340 (1991), factual data, numerical values, and discovered facts are fundamentally excluded from copyright protection:

Similarly, under the Canadian Copyright Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. C-42) and *CCH Canadian Ltd. v. ### 2. Zero Distribution of Proprietary Game Client Assets
Foreverbot strictly enforces an asset exclusion protocol:
- **No Client Binaries:** The repository contains zero Blizzard client executables (`WoW.exe`), dynamic link libraries (`.dll`), or binary runtime modules.
- **No Proprietary Archive Formats:** No Blizzard archive files (`.mpq`, `.casc`, `.idx`) or proprietary Blizzard Client Database files (`.dbc`, `.db2`) are stored, mirrored, or redistributed.
- **No 3D Meshes or Art Assets:** Zero proprietary 3D character models (`.m2`), world map terrain tiles (`.wmo`, `.adt`), textures (`.blp`), or user interface textures are hosted in this repository.
- **No Proprietary Audio:** Zero copyrighted sound effects, music tracks, voice acting files, or ambient cues are embedded or transmitted.

All data contained in Foreverbot consists exclusively of community-curated SQLite databases, public patch-note summaries, statistical calculations, and user-generated metadata.
