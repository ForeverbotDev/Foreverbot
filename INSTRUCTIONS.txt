  ⚔️ FOREVERBOT — QUICK START INSTRUCTIONS (NO CODING REQUIRED)
======================================================================

Thank you for downloading Foreverbot for World of Warcraft: Forever!
Everything you need (including the offline database) is bundled inside
this folder. Zero database setup or Docker required.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: GET YOUR FREE DISCORD BOT TOKEN (2 MINUTES)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Discord Developer Portal in your browser:
   https://discord.com/developers/applications
2. Click "New Application" (top-right), name it "Foreverbot", and click Create.
3. In the left menu, click "Bot" -> click "Reset Token" -> click "Copy".
4. In the left menu, click "OAuth2":
   - Under "Scopes", check:
     [x] bot
     [x] applications.commands
   - Under "Bot Permissions", check:
     [x] View Channels
     [x] Send Messages
     [x] Embed Links
     [x] Read Message History
     [x] Use Slash Commands
5. Copy the "Generated URL" at the bottom of the page, paste it into
   your browser, and invite the bot into your Discord server.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: DOUBLE-CLICK TO LAUNCH!
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply double-click the launch file for your operating system:

• 🐧 LINUX & 🍎 MACOS:
  Run or double-click "start_bot.sh"

• 💀 WINDOWS (PowerShell / Command Prompt):
  python bot.py

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: PASTE YOUR TOKEN & ENJOY!
----------------------------------------------------------------------
On your first double-click:
1. The script will automatically open Notepad (or your text editor)
   with your settings file.
2. Paste your token next to:
   DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN=your_token_here
3. Save the file and close the editor.
4. Double-click the launch file again—Foreverbot is LIVE!

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THAT'S IT!
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Foreverbot will appear online in your Discord server with all 41 slash
commands ready to use. Type "/help" in Discord to see the full list!

For boss guides, custom dungeon loot, and raid math, open README.md.
